# Rediscovering Relativity Is Not a Single Task

What It Takes for AI to Discover a Physical Theory

A position paper arguing that "can AI rediscover general relativity?" is not a
yes-or-no question. It reconstructs the relativity episode as a branching theory
space, decomposes rediscovery into a chain of tasks, and proposes three staged
tests that measure physics intelligence level by level: the Lorentz test, the
Poincaré test, and the gravity test.

## Where it is deposited

- Item: https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/id/eprint/30800
- Archive: https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/

PhilSci-Archive is the preprint archive of the Philosophy of Science
Association, hosted by the University of Pittsburgh. The deposit is a PDF; the
subject classification and keywords are entered in the archive's own form rather
than taken from the file.

Keywords used: AI for Science, LLMs, scientific discovery, physics intelligence,
relativity.
