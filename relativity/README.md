# Rediscovering Relativity Is Not a Single Task

What It Takes for AI to Discover a Physical Theory

A position paper arguing that "can AI rediscover general relativity?" is not a
yes-or-no question. It reconstructs the relativity episode as a branching theory
space, decomposes rediscovery into a chain of tasks, and proposes three staged
tests that measure physics intelligence level by level: the Lorentz test, the
Poincaré test, and the gravity test.
